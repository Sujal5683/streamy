DYNAMIC SELF-PROVISIONING (ANY USERNAME / EMAIL):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
You can also type ANY custom email or username in the "Operator Identity" box
(e.g., your personal work email: name@company.com).

The Control Plane automatically self-provisions that identity with full
Administrator permissions on the fly. You will NEVER be locked out or get
an HTTP 401 Unauthorized error!
